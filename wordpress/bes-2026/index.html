Smart Grids Meet Smart Storage

At Probus, we deploy smart meters that don't just measure energy, they create the intelligence layer powering India's grid evolution. Now, as energy storage becomes critical to grid stability and renewable integration, we're excited to participate in Bharat Electricity Summit (BES) 2026.

WHAT WE'RE BRINGING TO BES 2026

Insights on Smart Metering + Storage Integration

📡

Dual Communication (Cellular + BLE) and RF communication: Reliable and scalable communication infra for seamless meter data connectivity.

🖥

UHES (Unified Head End System): True to nature Unified HES enabling data onboarding from meters deployed on all type of communication technologies.

🛰

Probus Edgelync mini-FRTU: Our compact, high performance, feeder automation controller designed to deliver secure, real-time control across modern distribution networks.

⚡

DTMS: state if the art wireless Distribution Transformer Monitoring System for real-time transformer health monitoring and analytics.

🤖

GridSync: AI powered consumer to DT mapping solution for efficient mapping.
