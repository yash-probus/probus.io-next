One company making major waves in this sector is Probus Smart Things, a pioneering energy-tech startup that is deploying cutting-edge IoT solutions to transform how electricity is monitored, managed, and optimized. From smart metering to grid automation and solar plant performance, Probus is tackling the sector’s most pressing issues with innovative, real-world technology.

In this article, we explore how Probus is revolutionizing energy distribution through a holistic, scalable approach, and why their tech-first solutions are making utilities smarter and more sustainable.

1. The Digital Transformation of Utilities: A Necessity, Not a Luxury

Power utilities have historically relied on manual systems, legacy infrastructure, and reactive maintenance—leading to energy losses, inefficient grid operations, and limited consumer insight. As per estimates from the International Energy Agency (IEA), nearly 8% of generated electricity is lost during transmission and distribution globally. In countries with vast rural grids and infrastructural challenges like India, these losses can reach 15–20%.

This is where Probus’s integrated IoT-based platform plays a transformative role.

The Need for Smarter Grids

  • Energy Losses: Unmonitored grids lead to higher technical and commercial losses.
  • Limited Visibility: Traditional systems lack real-time analytics for utilities to monitor faults or consumption patterns.
  • Manual Dependency: Field inspections and meter readings are slow, error-prone, and resource-intensive.
  • Sustainability Goals: Achieving net-zero emissions requires optimized renewable energy integration and demand-side management.

2. Probus’s IoT Solutions: Intelligent, Interconnected, and Impact-Driven

Probus offers end-to-end, plug-and-play solutions that combine patented IoT hardware, edge intelligence, and AI-powered analytics to build smarter energy ecosystems. Their offerings fall into three key categories:

A. Smart Metering: Visibility and Control at Scale

Smart metering lies at the heart of a digital grid, and Probus’s metering solution provides real-time monitoring of power consumption and transmission, allowing utilities to proactively manage energy flow.

Key Technologies & Benefits:

  • NIC Cards with Multi-Protocol Support: Their patented communication modules support RF Mesh, NB-IoT, 4G, and BLE, ensuring secure and versatile data transmission.
  • Automated Meter Reading (AMR): Reduces dependency on manual meter reading by up to 95%, cutting operational costs and errors.
  • Consumer Load Analytics: Utilities can now detect abnormal consumption patterns, potential theft, and even predict peak load behavior using AI models.
  • Interoperability: Supports both single-phase and three-phase meters, allowing deployment across residential, commercial, and industrial zones.

Case in Point:

One utility using Probus’s solution reported a 22% reduction in T&D losses within six months of deployment, thanks to proactive grid monitoring and data-driven insights.

B. Solar Plant Monitoring: Maximizing ROI on Renewables

Solar energy is booming, but so are the inefficiencies from environmental factors like soiling, weather, and grid synchronization issues. Probus offers wireless monitoring solutions for large-scale solar plants, enabling asset managers to maximize performance and ROI.

What Sets It Apart:

  • Real-Time Soiling & Weather Impact Data: Track losses due to panel soiling, temperature, and humidity in real time.
  • Wireless Sensor Deployment: Reduces cabling and installation time by 40%, lowering setup costs.
  • Data-Driven O&M: Predictive maintenance and issue alerts help reduce downtime and improve energy generation.

Scalability:

Probus’s smart grid solutions are already deployed across over 500,000+ meters and sensors, with plans to expand into MENA and Southeast Asia. Their modular architecture allows utilities to start small and scale as needed.
